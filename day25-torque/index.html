Torque

A force can push an object forward, but it can also make an object rotate. Today, I explore torque—the rotational effect of a force— and how the point where a force is applied changes how easily something turns.

Calculus Connection

Torque measures the rotational effect of a force.

τ = rF sin(θ)

More generally, torque connects force to rotational motion and will lead into rotational dynamics.

What's Happening?

The same force can produce different amounts of rotation depending on where and how it is applied.

A force applied farther from the pivot produces more torque.
