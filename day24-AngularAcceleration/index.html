Angular Acceleration

Angular velocity tells us how quickly an object is rotating. But what happens when that rotational speed itself changes? Today, we take another derivative and explore angular acceleration.

Calculus Connection

Angular acceleration is the derivative of angular velocity:

α = dω/dt

Since ω = dθ/dt we can also write:

α = d²θ/dt²

What's Happening?

Angular acceleration describes how quickly the angular velocity changes.

A positive angular acceleration increases the angular velocity, while a negative angular acceleration decreases it.
